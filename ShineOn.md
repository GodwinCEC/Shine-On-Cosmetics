# ✨ Shine On Cosmetics - Website Guide

Welcome to your new digital home! This document is designed to help you navigate and manage the **Shine On Cosmetics** website with ease. It covers everything from how customers shop to how you manage the business from behind the scenes.

---

## 1. 🛍️ The Shopping Experience
Your customers enjoy a premium, editorial-style shopping experience. Here is where everything is:

*   **Home Page**: The "wow" factor. It features a stunning hero carousel, your best-selling collections, and heartwarming customer reviews.
*   **The Shop**: A dedicated space where customers can browse all products. They can filter by **Gender** (Female, Male, Unisex) or **Category** to find exactly what they need.
*   **Product Details**: When a customer clicks a product, they see beautiful high-res images, a detailed description, and the clear price.
*   **Order Tracking**: Customers can check the status of their orders at any time using their **Order Number** on the "Track Order" page.

---

## 2. 🛒 How Customers Order (The Flow)
We've made the checkout process as smooth as possible to ensure you don't lose sales:

1.  **Discovery**: Customer finds a product they love in the Shop.
2.  **Selection**: They add it to their bag (Cart).
3.  **Checkout**: They fill in their delivery details (Name, Phone, and Location). We've added specific options for campus hostels and general locations in Kumasi/beyond.
4.  **Payment**: They pay securely using **Paystack** (Mobile Money or Card).
5.  **Confirmation**: Once payment is successful, the order is created in your system, and the customer gets a "Thank You" confirmation with their order number.

---

## 3. 📊 The Admin Dashboard (Your Command Center)
This is where you run the show. You can access it by logging in and navigating to the Admin section.

### **Overview (The Big Picture)**
The moment you log in, you'll see your vital stats:
*   **Total Orders**: Every order ever placed.
*   **Expected vs. Paid Revenue**: See what's coming in versus what has been fully paid.
*   **Accepting Orders Toggle**: Found in the top right. If you're busy or out of stock, you can flip this switch to "Off" to pause new orders temporarily.

### **Order Management**
*   **View Details**: Click "Manage" on any order to see exactly what the customer bought and where it should be delivered.
*   **Update Status**: You can move orders from "Pending" to "Confirmed" once you've seen the payment, and finally to "Delivered" once the customer has their package.
*   **Filters**: Use the search bar or date filters to find specific orders from last week, last month, or a specific customer.

### **Product Management**
*   **Adding Products**: You can add new items with multiple images. The first image you upload will be the "cover" image.
*   **Visibility**: Want to hide a product without deleting it? Just use the "Visibility" toggle.
*   **Featured**: Flip the "Featured" switch to make a product show up on the Home Page's "Featured" section.
*   **Bulk Add**: If you have many products to add at once, use the "Bulk Add" button to speed things up.

### **Settings & Site Customization**
This is where you control the "brain" and the "look" of your website without needing any code.

*   **Store Status**:
    *   **Accepting Orders**: A secondary place to toggle your shop's availability.
    *   **Data Source**: A technical switch that determines if the site uses "Live" data or "Backup" data. *Tip: Keep this on "Live" unless advised otherwise!*
*   **Home Page Content**:
    *   **Hero Section**: Update the main welcome message (subtitle) that customers see first.
    *   **Founder & Mission**: You can update your Name, Bio, and "Our Story" text here. You can even change the mission emoji!
*   **Social Links**: Link your Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, and Snapchat. You can also hide specific social icons if you're taking a break from a platform.
*   **Save All Changes**: Always remember to click the big "Save All Changes" button at the top of the settings page after making updates!

## 4. 💳 Payment Processing with Paystack
Paystack is the engine that handles your money. It’s secure, fast, and trusted.

### **Getting Started**
*   **The Invitation**: I have sent a "Team Invitation" to your email. Please **open that email and click "Accept Invitation"**. This will give you access to your own Paystack dashboard.
*   **The Paystack Dashboard**: This is where you see the actual money. You can view successful transactions, see when your next payout is, and manage your bank account details.

### **How it Works**
*   **Customer Pays**: Money goes into your Paystack account immediately.
*   **Payouts**: Paystack usually sends the money to your registered bank account or Momo wallet on a **T+1 basis** (the next business day).
*   **Security**: You don't have to worry about handling sensitive card details; Paystack marks the order as "Paid" in your Admin Dashboard automatically once the transaction is successful.
